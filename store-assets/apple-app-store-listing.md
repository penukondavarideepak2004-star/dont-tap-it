Think fast. Tap smart. Can you survive the speed test?

**DON'T TAP IT!** is a sleek, ultra-responsive reaction game crafted for one-handed portrait play. Receive split-second instructions, recognize visual patterns, and tap the correct target before the timer runs out!

* ⚡ **Ultra Fast 60 FPS Engine**: Instant touch responsiveness with zero input lag.
* 🎯 **8 Unique Challenge Categories**: From color discrimination and size sorting to tricky cognitive negations.
* 🏆 **Daily Challenges & Streaks**: Unique daily seed shared across players globally.
* 🎨 **5 Customizable Themes**: Cyber Neon, Arcade Classic, Minimal Dark, Deep Space, and Sweet Candy.
* 🎵 **Synthesizer Sound & Haptics**: Crisp custom audio chords and tactile haptic feedback.
* 🔒 **Private & Offline**: Play offline without creating an account. Full data privacy.
