## Full Description
> Think fast. Tap smart. One mistake ends your run!

**DON'T TAP IT!** is the high-octane reaction and cognitive reflex game designed to test your focus, speed, and mental agility.

Read the rapid instructions and react before the timer expires:
* **TAP BLUE**
* **TAP THE BIGGEST**
* **TAP THE ODD SHAPE**
* **TAP THE MOVING ONE**
* **DON'T TAP RED** (Tap the only safe object!)
* **DON'T TAP ANYTHING!** (Resist the urge and wait for the clock!)

### 🔥 Key Features:
* **Instant Action**: Zero complicated tutorials. Pick up and play in 5 seconds.
* **8 Dynamic Challenge Types**: Color, Size, Odd One, Movement, Position, Count, Memory & Negation.
* **Speed Bonus & Combo Streaks**: Tap faster to multiply your score up to 2.0x!
* **Global Daily Challenge**: Play the same deterministic challenge sequence as players worldwide every day.
* **100% Offline Gameplay**: Play anywhere, anytime — no mandatory internet or logins.
* **Zero Dark Patterns**: Fair gameplay, optional rewarded second-chances, no forced deceptive ads.
* **Stunning Themes**: Unlock Arcade Classic, Cyber Neon, Minimal OLED Dark, Deep Space, and Sweet Candy.
* **Accessible & Color-Blind Friendly**: High-contrast geometric shapes with optional text labels.
